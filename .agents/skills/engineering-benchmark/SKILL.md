# Engineering Benchmark

## Purpose
Support prevalence/adoption claims with a deduplicated, auditable human evidence cohort rather than celebrity impressions.

## Activate when
Use for questions such as whether a practice is widespread, whether more than half of a defined cohort uses something, or comparisons requiring person-level direct-use evidence.

## Do not activate
Do not use as an objective ranking of intelligence or engineering worth, and do not use it for technical truth that source code/specification can settle directly.

## Antigravity-native execution
Maintain cohort registries/evidence in project data files; load only the relevant slice. Deduplicate identities by stable evidence, exclude bots/organizations and distinguish direct-use evidence from mentions.

## Workflow
1. Define cohort and qualifying evidence.
2. Verify unique identities and current technical relevance.
3. Bind each adoption vote to inspectable evidence.
4. Deduplicate aliases and repeated evidence.
5. Compute exact numerator/denominator.
6. Report unmet sample targets instead of padding.

## Validation
Never claim a 1000-person benchmark exists unless at least 1000 unique verified people are present. A majority of a verified 1000 requires at least 501 qualifying unique adopters.

## Boundaries
Rank is a search prior only. A lower-ranked subsystem maintainer can be the best source for a narrow proposition.
