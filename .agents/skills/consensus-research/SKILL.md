# Consensus Research

## Purpose
Measure genuine independent agreement without counting mirrors, reposts, vendor repetition or one source family as many votes.

## Activate when
Use for community experience, product/tool sentiment, recurring failure reports, operational best practices or prevalence questions.

## Do not activate
Do not use consensus to settle mathematics, source-code truth, law, specifications or other questions where primary authoritative evidence dominates.

## Antigravity-native execution
Build a small evidence ledger with source family, date, firsthand/secondhand status, proposition and independence. Use research tools available in the active runtime; keep bulky source lists outside the active skill text.

## Workflow
1. Define the proposition and target population.
2. Sample multiple independent source families.
3. Deduplicate copied/mirrored claims.
4. Separate firsthand use, hearsay, vendor claims and speculation.
5. Quantify agreement only from qualifying evidence.
6. Cross-check decision-critical claims against primary sources.

## Validation
Report numerator/denominator or qualitative confidence honestly. Do not claim a majority without a defined denominator and qualifying evidence.

## Boundaries
Popularity is not technical correctness. Community material can discover failure modes; authoritative implementation/state claims still require primary/runtime evidence.
