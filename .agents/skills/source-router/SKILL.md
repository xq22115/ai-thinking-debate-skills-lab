# Source Router

## Purpose
Route questions to the strongest current primary evidence and relevant technical owners rather than celebrity rank or generic popularity.

## Activate when
Use for AI engineering, agents, model systems, evals, RAG, memory, developer workflows, infrastructure or rapidly changing open-source ecosystems.

## Do not activate
Do not invoke a human/expert catalog when an exact specification, repository source or deterministic runtime observation already answers the question.

## Antigravity-native execution
Maintain curated source indexes in references/data, not the active prompt. Route by exact task/domain, canonical project source, current subsystem owner, identity certainty, what the artifact directly proves, freshness and transfer conditions. Rank is a discovery prior only.

## Workflow
1. Define the proposition and evidence needed.
2. Route to canonical implementation/spec first.
3. Add maintainers/specialists for design rationale and operational evidence.
4. Escalate to challenger/alternate ecosystems for gaps or counterevidence.
5. Record direct-proves vs do-not-infer boundaries.
6. Refresh stale lifecycle/revision evidence.

## Validation
A lower-ranked specialist may outrank a famous source for a narrow subsystem. Verify identity and immutable artifact/revision where material.

## Boundaries
Do not turn a curated pool into an objective ranking of people. Social popularity is discovery evidence, not implementation proof.
