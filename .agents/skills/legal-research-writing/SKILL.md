# Legal Research Writing

## Purpose
Produce clear legal analysis or correspondence without fabricating authorities, collapsing allegations into facts or hiding jurisdiction/time uncertainty.

## Activate when
Use for case/statute/regulation research, legal issue framing, formal legal correspondence, evidence-dense executive letters or legal-risk analysis.

## Do not activate
Do not present general writing advice as legal research, and do not infer legal conclusions when jurisdiction/material facts are missing.

## Antigravity-native execution
Resolve jurisdiction, relevant date, audience, requested deliverable and source requirements first. Use current primary legal authorities where available; store source notes separately from final prose. Re-check volatile law before relying on memory.

## Workflow
1. Separate verified facts, allegations, assumptions, law and inference.
2. Identify controlling jurisdiction and authority hierarchy.
3. Retrieve/verify primary authority and procedural posture.
4. Map propositions to citations and counterauthority.
5. Draft for the audience and requested strategic effect.
6. Run citation/fact consistency and adverse-reading review.

## Validation
Every material legal proposition must be traceable to authority or explicitly labeled analysis. Never invent cases, docket facts, quotations or holdings.

## Boundaries
This skill supports research/writing, not unauthorized practice or guaranteed legal outcomes. Preserve uncertainty and recommend professional review when stakes require it.
