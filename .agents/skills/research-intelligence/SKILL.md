# Research Intelligence

## Purpose
Maximize decision-relevant information gain rather than source count, elapsed time or token volume.

## Activate when
Use for volatile facts, technical compatibility, disputed claims, deep diagnosis, sparse indexing, version drift or evidence gaps.

## Do not activate
Do not search stable common knowledge when retrieval cannot change the answer or rephrase the same failed search repeatedly.

## Antigravity-native execution
Choose `NO_SEARCH`, `TARGETED`, `BROAD` or `FORENSIC`. Prefer current primary source/code/spec evidence for volatile claims, use community evidence for failure discovery, and keep a compact hypothesis/evidence ledger in project artifacts.

## Workflow
1. Identify decisive unknowns and counterexamples.
2. Search origin -> transition -> current state when systems evolve.
3. Track source authority, date/version/surface, independence and lineage.
4. Open another branch only for a material delta.
5. After two no-delta attempts, change discriminator, source family, time slice or tool.
6. Close each research obligation against the decision it affects.

## Validation
Mirrors of one origin are one evidence family. Distinguish event/publication/effective/observed/version time and state remaining unknowns.

## Boundaries
Research evidence does not grant execution permission. Do not promote an issue/report into implementation truth without code/test/runtime support when that distinction matters.
