## Validation policy
The archive intentionally distinguishes:
- artifact existence,
- static/local test success,
- files installed,
- host/client discovery,
- live product deployment,
- account-level installation,
- cross-platform compatibility,
- live-provider behavior,
- real-user semantic performance.

A previous chat saying “created” is never automatically upgraded to “installed”, “deployed”, “host verified”, or “live verified”.
